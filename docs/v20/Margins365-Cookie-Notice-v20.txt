Margins365 uses essential cookies and browser storage that are needed to sign you in, protect 
the site and make it work. These don't need consent under UK PECR and the EU ePrivacy rules.
We also use privacy-friendly analytics (Plausible Analytics) on our public pages, only if you 
click "Accept analytics". Plausible sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device; it doesn't 
store your IP address or follow you to other sites. If you reject, or close the banner, or your browser 
sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the analytics script is never loaded. We don't use advertising or 
cross-site tracking cookies.
