This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) forms part of the Margins365 Terms between the customer 
(“Customer”, the controller) and Margins365 (“Margins365”, the processor). It applies when 
Margins365 processes personal data on the Customer's behalf, and meets Article 28 of the UK GDPR 
and the EU GDPR. It takes effect when the Customer accepts the Terms.

2. Customer instructions
Margins365 processes personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions — these Terms, 
this DPA and the Customer's use of the service — unless the law requires otherwise, in which case it 
tells the Customer first unless the law forbids it. Margins365 tells the Customer if it believes an 
instruction breaks data protection law. The Customer is responsible for having a lawful basis and 
giving notices to its buyers.
3. Confidentiality and security
Everyone authorised to process the data is bound by confidentiality. Margins365 keeps appropriate 
technical and organisational measures, including: encryption in transit and at rest; removal of buyer 
contact details at import; deletion of original files after import; access limited to authorised personnel 
with two-step sign-in; hashed passwords and secure session cookies; rate limiting; logs without file 
contents or secrets; backups kept no longer than 7 days; regular deletion and restore tests.
4. Breaches
Margins365 notifies the Customer without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours, after 
becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Customer's data, with the information 
available, and updates it as more is known. It helps the Customer meet its own reporting duties.
5. Assistance and audits
Margins365 helps the Customer, taking into account the nature of the processing, to answer data 
subject requests, carry out data protection impact assessments and meet its security and breach 
obligations. It makes available the information needed to show compliance with this DPA and allows 
audits by the Customer or its auditor on 30 days' notice, once a year unless a breach or regulator 
requires more, under reasonable confidentiality.
6. Subprocessors
The Customer gives general authorisation for the subprocessors below. Margins365 will give at least 
30 days' notice of a new or replacement subprocessor by email to account owners and on this page; 
the Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds, and if no solution is found may 
cancel and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees. Margins365 puts equivalent data protection 
terms in place with each subprocessor and stays responsible for them.

7. Return and deletion
While access is active the Customer can download its data (CSV) at any time. When access ends 
(after cancellation at the trial or paid-period end, final failed payment or a first-payment refund), 
Margins365 deletes all Customer personal data in the service; backups are cleared within 7 days. 
Margins365 keeps only what the law requires it to keep, such as billing records, and uses that only for 
that purpose.
8. General
If this DPA conflicts with the Terms on data protection, this DPA wins. It is governed by the same law 
as the Terms.
