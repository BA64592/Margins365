This policy explains how Margins365 uses personal data, under the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 
2018 and, where it applies, the EU GDPR.
1. Who we are and how to contact us
Margins365 (“we”). We are the controller for the personal data described in section 3. For privacy 
questions or to use your rights, write to info.marginos@gmail.com or use the Contact us form at 
margins365.com/help. You do not need an account to contact us.
2. Our two roles
• Controller: for your account, team, billing, support and security data. 
• Processor: for personal data inside the shop files you upload. You (our business customer) are 
the controller of that data and our Data Processing Agreement applies.

6. Your rights
You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, and object to processing based on 
legitimate interests. Download my data (CSV) is in the app while your account is active. We reply 
within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests, with notice). Deletion has limits 
where we must keep records by law. If your data is in a customer's shop files, we will pass your 
request to that customer and help them answer it.
You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint) or, in the 
EU, your local data protection authority. We would like the chance to help first.
7. Cookies
We use essential cookies and browser storage needed to sign you in and run the app. On our public 
pages we use Plausible Analytics, which sets no cookies, only if you click "Accept analytics"; 
rejecting or closing the banner means it never loads, and you can change your choice at any time in 
Cookie settings. It is never used in the signed-in app. See our Cookie notice.
8. Security
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited to people who need it, passwords are 
hashed, and sign-in and forms are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you 
we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.
9. Children
Margins365 is for businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18.
10. Changes
We will post updates here with a new version number and date, and email account owners about 
significant changes.
