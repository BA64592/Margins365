K72 — Founding offer: 50% off for 6 months, first 100 paying users
Requirement.
• The first 100 paying accounts get 50% off for their first 6 monthly payments. From 
payment 7 the normal price applies. 
• Founding monthly prices: Starter £19.50, Growth £39.50, Pro £64.50, Scale £99.50 a 
month including VAT for 6 months, then £39 / £79 / £129 / £199. 
• Annual plans: the same value is given as 25% off the first year (half price for 6 of the 12 
months): £292.50, £592.50, £967.50, £1,492.50 for the first year, then £390 / £790 / 
£1,290 / £1,990. (Product decision: annual payments are yearly, so “6 months at half price” 
becomes 25% off the first annual payment.) 
• Counting. A place is reserved when a trial starts through Checkout and confirmed when the 
first payment succeeds. A reservation is released if the trial is cancelled, the first payment 
finally fails or the first payment is refunded. Reserved + confirmed never exceeds 100. One 
place per account. The counter lives on the server. 
• Plan changes during the 6 months keep 50% off the new plan until the 6 months end. 
Cancelling ends the offer; a later account does not get it again. 
• Honest display. While places remain, Pricing and Checkout say: “Founding offer: 50% off 
your first 6 months — for the first 100 paying customers. [n] places left.” The number is the 
real server count. When it reaches zero the offer disappears everywhere automatically. No 
countdown timers, no invented scarcity, no reset. 
• Disclosures. Checkout, the trial-started email, Plan & billing and the reminder show the 
founding amount, how many payments it lasts, the date the normal price starts and the normal 
amount. A “Founding price ends soon” email goes 14 days before the 6th month ends. 
• Stripe. A 50% coupon lasting 6 monthly invoices for monthly plans; a 25% one-off coupon for 
annual plans; applied server-side only when a place is reserved. Acceptance. FO-1 Only 100 
places can ever be confirmed, even with simultaneous Checkouts. FO-2 Payments 1–6 charge 
the founding amount; payment 7 the normal price. FO-3 Annual first year charges 75% of the 
annual price. FO-4 Released places return to the counter. FO-5 The displayed count equals the 
server count; the offer vanishes at zero. FO-6 All disclosures show founding amount, duration 
and normal price. FO-7 The ends-soon email is sent. v19 sections affected. 2, 3, 3.1, 3.2, 7, 
10.1 #11, 12.5, 12.10, 12.11 (S3, S24), 16.4, 16.5, 22.12 #8; K42 is superseded for the new 
offer only (the old 25% offer stays removed).

Part B — Product architecture (spec section 23, reproduced; B.n = spec 23.n)
Approved by Akthar Uddin, 11 November 2026 (Europe/London). Akthar asked for the product 
to be designed as a whole working product, not only a list of changes. Where his messages left gaps, 
the Business & Product Agent made the product decisions in this section under that instruction. These 
are requirements, not UI design: Design decides how screens look; this section fixes what the product 
does. Formulas in 12.0–12.3 are unchanged. K numbers refer to Appendix B.
B.0 Shared rules for every area
• Server is the source of truth. Plan, entitlement, role, shop, seat, founding-offer and trial 
state come from the server. The browser never decides access. Product data (orders, costs, 
ads, scenarios, results) is stored server-side per shop. Browser storage holds only UI 
preferences and a non-sensitive cache that is cleared on sign-out and at account closure (see 
23.13, finding F1). 
• Every screen has an address (K63). Refresh, back, forward and shared links reopen the 
same screen. Every navigation opens at the top of the new page (K62). Going back restores 
the previous scroll position, which is normal browser behaviour. 
• Time. Dates and times shown to customers use the account time zone and name it. Stored 
times are UTC. 
• Words. The product name is PRODUCT_NAME = Margins365 (K59). Plan names are Starter, 
Growth, Pro and Scale. 
• Accessibility and devices. Everything in this section meets 15.1–15.5 (WCAG 2.2 AA, 44 px 
targets, phone, iPad portrait and landscape, desktop). 
• Audit events. Owner actions that change billing, shops, seats, roles or the account write a 
privacy-safe audit event: type, time, account ID, actor user ID and plan. No message bodies, no 
card data.

B.2 Shops and multi-shop (K68)
Entity. Shop: id, account, name (1–60 characters, unique in the account), platforms enabled (within 
tier slots), currency, created, status active or deleting. Every order, cost, ad, overhead, saved dataset, 
scenario, result and upload belongs to exactly one shop. Settings that are per account: plan, billing, 
team, time zone. Settings that are per shop: platforms, currency, tax settings, target margin, FX 
settings.
Tier limits. Starter 1, Growth 1, Pro 1, Scale up to 3 (trial or paid). The monthly unique-order limit is 
account-wide on every plan (one bucket across all shops). Platform slots apply per shop (Starter 1, 
Growth up to 2, Pro and Scale all 3, while TikTok is coming soon).
Flows.
1. First shop. Created in first-run (23.5): name and platform. Required before upload. 
2. Add shop (Scale, owner). Account menu → Add shop, or Shops → Add shop → name, 
platforms → Create → the new shop opens empty with its own first-run upload prompt. At the 
limit the button is replaced by “Scale includes up to 3 shops” (no upgrade beyond Scale). 
3. Add shop on Starter, Growth or Pro (owner). The Add shop action is shown and opens an 
upgrade explainer: “Add up to 3 shops on Scale (£199/mo).” It never creates a second shop 
below Scale. 
4. Rename (owner). Inline; takes effect everywhere at once. 
5. Switch (Scale, everyone). From the account-button switcher or Shops. Switching changes 
the current shop in the address, keeps the same page type (for example Leaks → Leaks), and 
opens at the top. 
6. Remove (owner, Scale, when more than one shop). Shops → Remove → confirmation 
states the shop name, the item counts that will be deleted and “This can't be undone”; the 
owner types the shop name to confirm. The shop's product data is deleted (12.6 scope, 
backups within 7 days). Order usage already counted this billing month is not refunded. The 
last remaining shop cannot be removed; Start fresh (17.3) empties it instead. 
7. Combined view (Scale). The Control room offers “All shops” as a read-only combined 
summary: true profit, revenue and leak £ per shop side by side and in total. The Health Score 
stays per shop (it is not averaged), so formulas are unchanged. 
Downgrade from Scale. Applies at renewal (Q27). Before it applies, the owner chooses which one 
shop stays active. Other shops become locked (kept, not visible, not uploadable) and unlock on 
upgrade. If the owner does not choose, the oldest shop stays active. Locked shops are deleted only 
when access ends (K39).
Errors and edge cases. Duplicate name → inline error. Upload while a shop is being removed → 
refused with a clear message. A member removed mid-session loses access on next request. Two 
owners' tabs removing the same shop → second gets “This shop has already been removed”.
Acceptance. SH-1 Starter/Growth/Pro cannot create a second shop by any route (UI or API). SH-2 
Scale can create, rename, switch and remove up to 3 shops. SH-3 Data never appears in another shop 
(orders, costs, results, exports, PDF, search). SH-4 The order limit is counted once across all shops; re-
uploading the same orders to the same shop does not add usage. SH-5 Removing a shop deletes its 
data and keeps usage; the last shop cannot be removed. SH-6 Switching survives refresh. SH-7 Scale 
downgrade locks, not deletes, extra shops; upgrade restores them.

B.9 Trial lifecycle (K69, K72)
States: none → checkout_open → trialing (day 1–14) → active (day 15 charge succeeded) or past_due 
(charge failed; retries up to 14 days) or cancel_at_period_end (owner cancelled; access to the original 
trial end) → ended (closed and wiped). One trial per account; per-card prevention where Stripe supports 
it (recommended default).
Rules. The trial runs on the plan the customer chose (K69): Starter trial = Starter features and 500 
orders; Growth = Growth and 2,000; Pro = Pro and 5,000; Scale = Scale and 15,000. Changing plan 
during the trial keeps the same trial end, creates no new trial and charges nothing today. Day 15 
charges the chosen plan's price, or the founding price if the account holds a founding place (K72).
Emails: trial started (plan, amount, first charge date, founding price and end date if applicable, how 
to cancel in Plan & billing); reminder 3 days before the end (default, configurable) with the same facts 
and a link to Plan & billing; first payment receipt (Link); payment failed; cancellation confirmation.

Acceptance. TR-1 Each tier's trial grants exactly that tier. TR-2 Day 15 charges the displayed amount

stored) and alert the admin; retry sending.
The form is not a cancellation route; if the message mentions cancelling, the copy email includes one 
line: “To cancel, the account owner uses Plan & billing.”
Stored messages are viewable in an authenticated admin inbox (owner of the business operating 
Margins365 only) and are deleted 12 months after receipt.

B.18 Analytics and the consent banner (K79)
Approved by Akthar, 11 November 2026, 14:38 Europe/London.
Provider: Plausible Analytics (Plausible Insights OÜ, Estonia). Chosen because it sets no cookies 
and uses no browser storage, stores no IP addresses or persistent identifiers (a daily-rotating 
salted hash counts unique visitors), keeps all visitor data in the EU (Hetzner, Germany; UpCloud, 
Finland; Bunny CDN, Slovenia) and provides a DPA to every customer (Plausible DPA; EU hosting; 
compliance). Margins365 is the controller; Plausible is its processor.
Gating (stricter than the law requires, by Akthar's instruction). The analytics script is not 
loaded and makes no request until the visitor clicks Accept analytics. This works the same for UK 
and EU visitors. Reject or no choice means no analytics. A Global Privacy Control signal (Sec-GPC: 1) is 
treated as Reject: the banner is not shown and nothing loads, though the visitor can still opt in 
through Cookie settings.
Consent banner.
• Shown on the first visit to any public page, at the bottom, without covering the main heading 
or blocking use of the page (no cookie wall). 
• Text: "We'd like to use privacy-friendly analytics (no cookies, no tracking across sites) to see 
which pages help people. You can change this at any time in Cookie settings." with a link to the 
Cookie notice. 
• Two buttons of equal size, weight and prominence: "Accept analytics" and "Reject". No 
pre-ticked boxes; closing the banner (X or Escape) counts as Reject. 
• The choice is stored in browser local storage as m365_consent (value, notice version, date). 
Storing the choice is strictly necessary and is listed in the Cookie notice. It is asked again after 
12 months or when the Cookie notice version changes. 
• Cookie settings link in the footer (Legal column) reopens the choice at any time; withdrawing 
is as easy as giving consent and takes effect immediately (the script is removed and no further 
events are sent). 
• Keyboard and screen-reader operable, focus not trapped, WCAG 2.2 AA, 44 px targets; doesn't 
cause layout shift (CLS < 0.1). 
• Signed-in users see the banner only on public pages. 
What is measured. Public pages only: /, /sample-shop, /pricing, /help and help articles, legal pages, 
the calculators and landing pages. No analytics in the signed-in app (/app/*, /account/*, /welcome, 
/invite/*), on sign-in or reset pages, or in emails. Page views use the canonical URL with no query 
string. Events (no personal data, no entered numbers): Calculator used (property: calculator name), 
Start trial clicked, Sample shop opened, Contact form sent, Checkout started. Never sent: email, names, 
business names, account or shop IDs, invite or reset tokens, form contents, calculator inputs.
Data and retention. Aggregate statistics only. Margins365 deletes site statistics older than 24 
months (manual yearly task until automated). Plausible is added to the Privacy Policy recipients and 
to the DPA page's list of service providers for Margins365's own website (it does not process Customer 
shop data).
Legal documents. The Cookie notice, Privacy Policy and DPA & subprocessors page move to v20.1 
(approved texts in /workspace/v20/), published in the same release that turns analytics on. Until then 
the site shows v20.
Acceptance. AN-1 No request to Plausible (or any analytics host) before Accept, after Reject, with 
GPC, or on signed-in pages. AN-2 After Accept, page views and the listed events arrive in Plausible 
with no personal data or entered values. AN-3 Withdrawal stops sending immediately. AN-4 Banner: 
equal choices, no pre-ticks, close = reject, re-ask rules, accessible. AN-5 No cookies are set by 
analytics. AN-6 v20.1 legal texts published with the release.
