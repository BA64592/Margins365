Margins365 — Refunds and cancellation policy
Version 20 · Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Approved
This policy explains how cancelling and refunds work for Margins365. It forms part of our Terms.
1. The free trial
Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial of the plan you choose. You add a card at checkout and pay 
nothing today. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, your chosen plan is charged 
automatically on day 15 — the normal price, or the founding price if you hold a founding place 
(50% off your first 6 monthly payments, or 25% off the first year on an annual plan). We email you a 
reminder before the first charge, showing the amount and date.
2. How to cancel
Only the account owner can cancel.
1. Sign in and open Plan & billing. 
2. Choose Cancel trial or Cancel subscription. 
3. Check the date shown and, if you want, download your data. You can tell us why you are 
leaving — this is optional. 
4. Choose Confirm cancel. 
That is all. We email a confirmation with the key dates. We do not accept cancellation by email or 
phone, and you never need to contact us to cancel. If you cancel through Link's subscription tools 
instead, we treat it the same way.

When access ends, your login is removed and all product data (orders, costs, results, shops, team 
links and settings) is permanently deleted. Backups are cleared within 7 days. Download your data 
before the date shown. Until then you can undo the cancellation in Plan & billing. A later sign-up is a 
new, empty account and does not get the founding offer again.
4. Refunds
• First payment: you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of your first successful payment — 
including when that payment is the first charge after your free trial. Once per customer. 
• How to ask: use the Contact us form in the Help centre (margins365.com/help) with your 
account email and the payment date. Do not send card details. 
• What happens: we refund to the original payment method within 14 days of agreeing the 
refund. Access ends at once and your data is deleted, so download it first. 
• Later payments: not refunded in part, including when you cancel part-way through a period. 
You keep access until the period ends. 
• Founding places: refunding the first payment releases the founding place. 
5. Your legal rights
Margins365 is a service for businesses. If the law treats you as a consumer, you also have the 
statutory rights that apply where you live, including any right to cancel a distance contract within 14 
days and remedies if the service is not provided with reasonable care and skill. You can use those 
rights by sending a clear statement through the Help centre contact form or at 
info.marginos@gmail.com. This policy does not reduce those rights.
6. Questions
Contact us through the Help centre form or at info.marginos@gmail.com. These channels are not a 
way to cancel — the owner cancels in Plan & billing.
