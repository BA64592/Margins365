Answers from existing policy (no decision needed)
• Status labels: GA-01, GA-02, GA-17, GA-21, GA-28, GA-29 and GA-32 are only "Fixed" once 
QA has passed them with evidence. That means Stripe test-mode dashboard checks, a 
received email and a second-device check. Until then, mark them "Built, awaiting QA". 
• GA-32 SES production proven: this needs screenshots of production access, a verified 
identity, DKIM and MAIL FROM, plus a test email received at a non-team address. 
• GA-55: every "Cancel before day 14 and pay nothing" wording must go. Replace it with 
"Cancel before your trial ends (date shown at Checkout and in Plan & billing)". 
• GA-28: the contact pop-up, the line under the button and the visitor confirmation email must 
read exactly "Thank you for contacting us, we aim to respond within 24 hours." The visitor's 
confirmation must not include their message text. 
• GA-40 to GA-44: product data must be stored on the server for each shop; the browser may 
only cache it. Order limits are enforced on the server. Data is wiped at the end of access, with 
backups cleared within 7 days, and the account then closes. The Privacy Policy wording only 
becomes true once this is built. 
• Release rule: no screen or email may show success unless the server, and Stripe where 
money is involved, confirms it. 
• Fix order: all P0s first, then the P1s, then a mobile re-test (GA-64). Release only when no P0 
or P1 is open.
