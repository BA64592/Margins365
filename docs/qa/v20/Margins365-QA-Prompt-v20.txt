1. Your brief (read this first)
You are the QA & Testing team for Margins365, a subscription web app at 
https://margins365.com. It shows online sellers (Shopify, Amazon, Etsy) their true profit, a Health 
Score from 0 to 100, and the margin leaks that cost them most, ranked in pounds.
Your job is to prove, with evidence, that every feature in the spec has been built and works 
end to end. That covers the public site, sign-in, checkout and billing, shops and seats, imports, the 
calculations, the Control room, cancellation and data deletion, emails, the legal documents, every 
device size and security. Do not assume something works because it shows on screen. Many features 
can look finished while the server does nothing behind them.
How to work
1. Test against the spec, not against the current build. Where the build and this prompt 
disagree, raise a defect. Do not "fix" the expected result to match the build. 
2. Evidence for every result. Each Pass or Fail needs a screenshot or video, the URL, the 
device and browser, the account used and the time (Europe/London). A Pass with no evidence 
counts as Not run. 
3. Check what happens behind the screen. For anything involving money, limits, permissions 
or deletion, confirm what the server and Stripe did (Stripe test dashboard, the email received, 
the database or admin view, network requests). A message on screen is not proof. 
4. Try to break it. Every suite includes boundary, error and abuse cases. Run them all, not just 
the happy path. 
5. Never test on real customer data or real cards. Use Stripe test mode and the test 
accounts in section 5. The only live-mode checks are the low-risk ones in suite CHK, run by 
Akthar or with his written go-ahead. 
6. Do not change the spec or contact customers. Raise questions through the defect log, 
tagged SPEC-QUESTION. 
7. Use plain English in defect reports. Each report should make sense to someone who has not 
seen the screen. 
Result codes: Pass · Fail · Blocked (the test can't run, give the reason) · Not applicable (give the 
reason).
